Branch Desk: proposal for stakeholder review

A one-page tool for branch tellers: fill in a currency exchange, check it against head-office rules, and print a record to email to head office. It never moves money.

Prototype. Everything here uses sample data and interim rules. It is not an Alterna Savings service. Do not enter real member details.

Try the prototype

The real validation and rules engine, running in your browser with a sample rate. Try 1,500.00 CAD (within limits), 1,500.01 CAD (cannot be done at the branch), or a weekend time.
