## Headline findings

- **Printing from a dark-mode computer probably gives an unreadable record (DESK-UI-01, High).** The print override `:root{...}` (specificity 0,1,0) loses to the dark token blocks `:root:not([data-theme="light"])` and `:root[data-theme="dark"]` (0,2,0). The print block also never resets `--good`, `--bad`, `--review`, `--warn`, `--brand` or the tints. As a result, the reason and next-step box is printed (forced by `print-color-adjust: exact`) on `#17301F`/`#3A1B19`/`#2A2D55` behind black body text (about 1.5:1), and the stamp prints in pastel `#6FD08F`/`#FF8F85`/`#AEB8FF` on white paper (1.9 to 2.2:1). Whether this happens depends on whether the browser keeps `prefers-color-scheme: dark` while printing. **Verify** in Chrome, Edge and Safari. The fix is a few lines of CSS.
- **The form follows Pass 4 closely.** It uses the control tokens, a 24px radio with an ink dot, a `fieldset`/`legend` group, a `currentColor` chevron with no image request, a vertical-only textarea, and `aria-describedby` with `aria-invalid` and no per-field `role="alert"`. There are no inline styles, orange is used only in the wordmark, and every status is shown as shape plus word plus colour. One gap: **the error summary's items are not links to their fields (DESK-UI-04)**.
- **Focus is lost on server and network failures (DESK-UI-05).** The whole fieldset is disabled while the request is being checked, so focus drops to `<body>`. Only the validation path moves focus back to the summary.
- **Copy conflict that needs a decision (DESK-UI-06).** Under trial rules, a "Within branch limits" record says "You may complete the exchange", while the trial band says "Head-office confirmation required". The product owner must decide whether the teller waits for head office.
- **Alterna re-check: no change since 2026-10-02 (DESK-UI-16).** The bundle version is still `v124765`, and the `:root` tokens and form rules match. The logo is an SVG at `/media/t0onoi0m/alterna-savings.svg`. It is not used, and it was not downloaded or reproduced. Using it needs Alterna's sign-off and assets (DESK-UI-17, DL-N37).
