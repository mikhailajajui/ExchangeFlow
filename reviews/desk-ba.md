## Headline findings

- **The tool lets placeholder rules decide in production.** Real members make this production. `rules.ts` marks every rule `nonProduction` while `status` is `TRIAL`, but the desk calls the engine directly, so the production guard in `app-services` (services.ts:168, INV-PLACEHOLDER-1) never runs. A "Within branch limits" record then tells the teller "You may complete the exchange". This conflicts with ADR-021 (Accepted). Before any use with members, the owner must either restrict what a `TRIAL` result allows or supersede ADR-021 (DL-N41).
- **The page contradicts itself on when confirmation happens.** The banner says "Every record must be confirmed by head office". The within-limits next step says to complete first and email afterwards. The printed reason says "Approved automatically under current policy." directly under a band that says "not approved by head office". Nobody has defined what "confirmation" is, who gives it, how fast, or what happens if none arrives (DL-N43).
- **There is no register.** Record numbers are random, and the desk keeps no copy, by design. Head office cannot tell whether a record is missing, was re-checked, or was split into smaller amounts. The emailed PDFs, which carry the member's name, sit in mailboxes and Downloads folders with no retention rule (DL-N39 is still open; DL-N44 and DL-N45 are new).
- **Only one of the three limit fields changes any outcome.** Anything above `branchLimit` already goes to head-office review, and validation keeps `branchLimit` at or below `absoluteBranchMax`. So `headOfficeReviewAbove` and `absoluteBranchMax` change no outcome, and an officer will think they do. The fields an officer would expect (hours, holidays, per-branch limits, effective date, per-member daily limit) are missing (DL-N49).
- **Copy follows the banned-word and raw-code rules**, which tests cover. But the action after a decline is not named "Escalate" (DL-N31), "Approved" appears on a trial record, and the estimate says the amount is "fixed" while the record calls it an estimate.
